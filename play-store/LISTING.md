```
Kharcha builds your spending history automatically from the payment messages already on your phone, and keeps all of it on your phone.

WORKS OFFLINE, STAYS PRIVATE
• Kharcha has no internet permission, so Android itself stops it from going online
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics
• Your data is stored only on this phone and is never uploaded or backed up to a cloud
• Check it yourself: Settings › Apps › Kharcha › Permissions shows no network access

TRACKS SPENDING FOR YOU
• Picks up UPI, card, net banking and wallet payments from your messages
• Skips OTPs, offers, payment requests, reminders and failed payments
• Merges the same payment reported twice
• Anything it isn't sure about goes to "Needs review" instead of being guessed

SEE WHERE IT WENT
• This month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, all time or your own dates
• Total spent, income and savings, with the change from the previous period
• Spending by category and over time, and your top places to spend

EASY TO FIX
• Change amount, payee, category or date, and see the original message
• "Always put Swiggy in Food" rules that also fix past entries
• Swipe to mark something as not an expense
• Add cash expenses by hand

PDF REPORTS
• Export any date range as a PDF, made on your phone
• Save it or share it only when you choose

IN ENGLISH AND HINDI
• Switch language any time in Settings

Made in India.
```

## App content answers

**Privacy policy:** the public link to the privacy policy page.

**Ads:** No, the app contains no ads.

**App access:** All functionality is available without special access (no login).

**Content rating:** Utility / productivity app. Answer "No" to violence, sexual content, language, controlled substances, gambling and user interaction. There's no user-generated content or sharing between users.

**Target audience:** 18 and over.

**News app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Health app:** No.

**Financial features:** Kharcha only tracks and summarises the user's own spending. It doesn't
offer loans, payments, money transfers, banking, trading or insurance, and it never moves money.
If the form lists a budgeting or personal-finance-tracking option, choose that; otherwise choose
that the app provides none of the listed financial features.

### Data safety

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No**
- (Because the app has no network access, nothing is "collected" in Google's sense: data processed
  only on the device doesn't count as collection.)
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Not applicable (nothing is sent).
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Not applicable, but you
  can note that Settings › Delete all data from this phone erases everything.

### Sensitive permissions: SMS declaration

**Core functionality:** SMS-based money management

**Why the app needs READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS** (paste into the declaration form):

```
Kharcha is an offline personal expense tracker. Its core feature is building the user's spending
history automatically from the payment messages banks and UPI apps send to the phone. READ_SMS
is used once, after the user turns on auto-tracking, to read existing payment messages;
RECEIVE_SMS adds new payment messages as they arrive. Messages are parsed on the device only:
the app has no INTERNET permission, and nothing is uploaded or shared. Non-payment messages
(OTPs, promotions, personal chats) are skipped and not stored. Users who decline the permission
can still add expenses manually.
```
